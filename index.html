Industry outlook

What the market is telling us.

The day’s signal is that AI demand is increasingly constrained by the physical and financial systems around the models. Accelerator supply is only one bottleneck: high-bandwidth memory, advanced packaging, optical networking, transformers, switchgear, liquid-cooling equipment, and utility interconnections can each slow a new build. Geopolitical exposure remains material through chip-export controls, regional power policy, and the location of advanced manufacturing. As projects grow, financing discipline matters too—utilization assumptions, power contracts, and construction schedules now influence which announced data-center capacity actually reaches service.
